# Lead85MKII cabinet captures

The four factory WAVs are guitar cabinet captures by Jester Dyne Productions. The original pack handbooks release them under CC0. The factory copies are 44.1 kHz, capped at 200 ms with a short tail fade and stored as 16-bit PCM. The original downloads include full-length 44.1 and 48 kHz files.

| Factory selection | Original patch | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V30 / SM57 | 1 – Cookie Monster | [Brutal IR Pack](https://www.jester-dyne-productions.com/brutal-ir-pack/) |
| V30 / SM57 alternate | 12 – World Collider | [Brutal IR Pack](https://www.jester-dyne-productions.com/brutal-ir-pack/) |
| Greenback / SM57 | 1 – Nacho Guacamole | [Emerald IR Pack](https://www.jester-dyne-productions.com/emerald-ir-pack/) |
| Greenback / e606 | 4 – Pesto Paladin | [Emerald IR Pack](https://www.jester-dyne-productions.com/emerald-ir-pack/) |

Use **Load WAV...** to load a mono or stereo cabinet response up to one second and under 2 MB. The plug-in resamples at the host rate, matches gain across seven guitar midrange frequencies, preserves IR timing and stereo balance, and embeds the WAV in the session. **CAB** bypasses the speaker stage for an external cabinet processor.
